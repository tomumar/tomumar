The Story Behind 10+ Years of Excellence

Learn about my journey, my philosophy, my core values, and the principles that drive every marketing campaign I build for my clients. This page tells the full story of who I am and how I work.

I am Tom Umar, a Book Marketing Specialist Expert who has spent over a decade helping authors across every genre and publishing path achieve meaningful, lasting success in a rapidly evolving marketplace.

My journey into book marketing began with a simple yet powerful observation: talented authors were writing incredible books, but far too many of those books were never reaching the readers who would love them. The publishing landscape can be overwhelming, filled with conflicting advice, expensive guesswork, and strategies that simply do not deliver results. I set out to change that.

Over the past 10+ years, I have had the privilege of working with debut authors launching their very first title, mid-career authors looking to reignite their sales, and established names seeking to expand into new markets and audiences. I have marketed books across more than 15 genres, managed over $500,000 in advertising spend, and helped generate more than 1,000,000 copies sold across all my campaigns combined. Every single project has reinforced my belief that great marketing is not about hype or empty promises. It is about understanding your book, identifying your ideal readers, and building a strategic, data-informed campaign that connects the two in a way that feels authentic and drives real, measurable sales.

What sets my approach apart from other book marketers is a deep commitment to three core principles that guide every decision I make:

  • Precision Over Guesswork

    Every campaign I build is rooted in market research, competitive analysis, and audience data. I do not rely on trends or assumptions. I study what works in your specific genre, test rigorously, and optimize continuously to ensure your marketing budget is working as hard as possible. I track every metric that matters and make data-driven decisions at every stage of the campaign.

  • Transparency at Every Step

    You will always know exactly what I am doing, why I am doing it, and what results it is generating. I provide detailed reports, clear explanations, and honest assessments at every stage. No jargon, no hidden costs, no surprises, no vague promises. You will see exactly where every dollar goes and what return it generates. If something is not working, I will tell you immediately and adjust the strategy accordingly.

  • Email-First Communication

    I communicate with all clients primarily through email. This is a deliberate and strategic choice, not a limitation. Email communication ensures that every strategy discussion, every piece of feedback, every decision, and every report is documented, underlined, and recorded for future reference. You will always have a complete, searchable record of our entire working relationship that you can reference at any point in time. Phone or video calls are reserved for particular occasions when real-time conversation is genuinely necessary and adds clear value beyond what email can provide.

    • My mission is straightforward: to ensure that every author I work with sees real, measurable, and lasting results from their marketing investment. Your book deserves to be read by the people who will love it, and I am here to make that happen through strategic, transparent, and meticulously documented work.

    My Journey

    A Decade of Growth, Learning, and Results

    Every year of my career has brought new challenges, new insights, and new opportunities to refine my craft. Here are some of the key milestones that have shaped who I am as a marketer.

    2015

    The Beginning

    I began my career in book marketing after spending years observing the struggles authors faced in getting their work noticed. My first campaigns were small, focused, and scrappy. I learned everything I could about Amazon's algorithms, social media marketing, and audience building. Those early experiences taught me the importance of data, testing, and relentless optimization. Even with limited budgets, I was able to achieve meaningful results that validated my approach and fueled my passion for this work.

    2017

    First Bestseller Achievement

    I helped my first author reach a major bestseller list, proving that strategic, data-driven marketing could compete with and even outperform the marketing budgets of major publishing houses. This milestone was a turning point. It showed me and my clients that the right strategy, executed with precision and discipline, can produce extraordinary results regardless of the size of the budget or the author's existing platform.

    2019

    Expanding Expertise and Reach

    By this point, I had successfully marketed books across more than 10 genres and developed deep expertise in Amazon advertising, email marketing, and social media strategy. I refined my email-first communication model after realizing that clients who had everything documented in writing consistently had better experiences, fewer misunderstandings, and stronger working relationships. This became a permanent pillar of my practice.

    2021

    50+ Authors, $300K+ in Managed Ad Spend

    I crossed the milestone of having worked with over 50 authors and managed more than $300,000 in advertising spend. My processes became more refined, my strategies more sophisticated, and my results more consistent. I began developing proprietary frameworks for book launch campaigns, backlist revitalization, and long-term author brand building that would become the foundation of my current methodology.

    2023

    1 Million Copies Sold Milestone

    The combined sales across all my campaigns surpassed 1,000,000 copies sold. This was not just a number. It represented thousands of readers connecting with stories and knowledge that changed their lives, entertained them, and inspired them. It also represented the trust that over 100 authors placed in me to handle one of the most important aspects of their creative careers.

    2026

    Today and Beyond

    Today, I continue to work with select authors who are serious about achieving exceptional results. My methods have been refined through thousands of campaigns, hundreds of A/B tests, and an unwavering commitment to staying at the absolute forefront of book marketing best practices. I am constantly learning, constantly testing, and constantly pushing the boundaries of what is possible in this industry. The best is always yet to come.

    The book marketing landscape changes rapidly, and I am committed to staying at the cutting edge. I invest heavily in ongoing education, attend industry conferences, study platform algorithm changes, and continuously refine my strategies based on the latest data and trends.

    Uncompromising Quality

    I would rather turn down a project than deliver mediocre work. Every ad I write, every email I craft, every strategy I develop meets my personal standard of excellence. I take enormous pride in the quality of my work, and that pride shows in the results I deliver.

    Who I Work With

    Authors Across Every Publishing Path

    Whether you are self-published, traditionally published, or hybrid, I bring the same level of expertise and dedication to your marketing campaign.

    Self-Published Authors

    Self-published authors have complete creative control but often lack the marketing infrastructure that traditional publishers provide. I fill that gap with comprehensive strategies that cover everything from metadata optimization and Amazon advertising to social media marketing and email list building. You get the full marketing department experience without giving up any creative control or royalties.

    • Full-service marketing without publisher overhead
    • Maximum ROI from every marketing dollar spent
    • Complete creative control maintained throughout
    • Build a sustainable independent author business

    Traditionally Published Authors

    Even with a traditional publisher behind you, their marketing support is often limited, especially for midlist authors. I work alongside your publisher's efforts to amplify results, fill gaps in their marketing plan, and ensure your book gets the attention it truly deserves. My strategies complement and enhance whatever your publisher is already doing without any conflict or overlap.

    • Supplement limited publisher marketing budgets
    • Independent strategies that complement publisher efforts
    • Build your personal brand beyond your publisher's reach
    • Strengthen your position for future contract negotiations

    Hybrid Published Authors

    Hybrid authors face unique challenges that require a marketer who understands both the traditional and self-publishing worlds. I create unified strategies that account for the specific dynamics of hybrid publishing, ensuring consistency across all platforms and maximizing the unique advantages that the hybrid model offers. My approach bridges both worlds seamlessly.

    • Unified strategy across traditional and self-pub titles
    • Maximize advantages of the hybrid model
    • Consistent branding and marketing across all titles
    • Strategic guidance on future publishing decisions

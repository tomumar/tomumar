Case Study 01

Author S: Debut Psychological Thriller

GenrePsychological Thriller
Publishing PathSelf-Published
Starting Budget$3,000
Campaign Duration90 Days

The Challenge

Author S came to me with a completed debut novel and absolutely no existing audience, no email list, no social media following, and no industry connections. The psychological thriller market is extremely competitive, with thousands of new titles launching every month. Author S had a limited budget of $3,000 for the entire marketing campaign. The goal was ambitious but clear: achieve bestseller status and build a foundation for a long-term author career.

The Strategy

  • Complete metadata overhaul with optimized keywords and strategic category placement
  • Targeted Amazon Sponsored Products campaign focusing on comp author keywords
  • BookTok influencer outreach campaign with 15 targeted creators in the thriller niche
  • ARC team of 50 readers coordinated for launch week review surge
  • Email list built from scratch using a free prequel short story as lead magnet
  • Coordinated launch week pricing strategy with BookBub submission
4,200Copies Sold in 30 Days
USA TodayBestseller List Achieved
6.8xReturn on Ad Spend
2,100Email Subscribers Built
Case Study 02

Author J: Business and Productivity Non-Fiction

GenreBusiness / Productivity
Publishing PathHybrid Published
Starting Budget$5,000
Campaign Duration6 Months

The Challenge

Author J had published a business book through a hybrid publisher but sales had plateaued at approximately 50 copies per month after an underwhelming launch. The book had strong content and excellent reviews, but it was invisible in a crowded market. Author J needed a strategy to dramatically increase visibility and establish long-term, sustainable sales growth rather than another short-lived sales spike.

The Strategy

  • Complete metadata overhaul resulting in immediate organic visibility improvements
  • Amazon advertising campaign with deep keyword research targeting business readers
  • LinkedIn content strategy positioning Author J as a thought leader in the productivity space
  • Email list building with a free productivity toolkit as lead magnet
  • Podcast outreach campaign targeting 40+ business and productivity shows
  • Monthly A/B testing on ad creatives, email subjects, and landing page copy

Case Study 03

Author E: Contemporary Romance Series

GenreContemporary Romance
Publishing PathTraditionally Published
Starting Budget$8,000
Campaign Duration4 Months

The Challenge

Author E was an established traditionally published romance author with 12 backlist titles, but a new release was underperforming compared to previous books. The publisher's marketing support was minimal, and Author E needed an independent marketing strategy to revitalize both the new release and the entire backlist catalog. The ambitious goal was to achieve a number one ranking in the Contemporary Romance category on Amazon.

The Strategy

  • Aggressive Amazon advertising campaign across Sponsored Products and Lockscreen
  • BookTok and Bookstagram influencer partnerships with 25 romance content creators
  • Backlist cross-promotion strategy linking all 12 titles in a discovery funnel
  • Email marketing campaign to existing reader base with exclusive content and bonuses
  • BookBub Featured Deal secured for the new release
  • Blog tour with 20 romance-focused book bloggers and reviewers
8,500Copies Sold in 2 Weeks
#1Contemporary Romance Ranking
8.3xReturn on Ad Spend
45%Backlist Sales Increase

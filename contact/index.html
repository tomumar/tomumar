Why Email-First?

All communication is conducted primarily through email to ensure that every strategy discussion, recommendation, and decision is fully documented, underlined, and recorded for future reference. This approach provides complete transparency and accountability for both of us throughout our working relationship.

What to Include in Your Message

The more detail you provide, the more helpful and specific my initial response will be. Here is what I would love to know:

  • Your book's title and genre so I can begin researching the competitive landscape immediately.
  • Your publishing status including whether you are traditionally published, self-published, hybrid, or still in the writing/planning stage.
  • Your primary marketing goals such as hitting a bestseller list, increasing monthly sales, building an email list, or launching a new release.
  • Your approximate budget range so I can tailor my recommendations to strategies that make financial sense for your situation.
  • Any previous marketing efforts including what you have tried before, what worked, and what did not. This helps me avoid repeating past mistakes.
  • Your timeline and urgency including any upcoming release dates, promotional events, or deadlines you are working toward.
  • Your existing platform including social media following, email list size, website traffic, and any other marketing assets you already have in place.
  • What success looks like to you because every author defines success differently, and I want to make sure my strategy aligns with your personal vision.

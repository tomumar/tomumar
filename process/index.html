My Proven 5-Step Process

Every successful book marketing campaign follows a structured, repeatable, and thoroughly documented process. Here is exactly how I work with each author, from our very first email exchange to the ongoing optimization that keeps your book selling month after month, year after year.

Why Process Matters

Structure Creates Consistent Results

In my 10+ years of marketing books, I have learned that the difference between a campaign that delivers exceptional results and one that falls flat almost always comes down to process. Creativity matters, strategy matters, but without a disciplined, structured process to execute that strategy, even the best ideas fail to deliver their full potential.

That is why I have developed and refined a proven 5-step process that guides every project I take on. This process ensures that nothing falls through the cracks, every decision is backed by data and research, every action is documented via email for your records, and every result is measured and optimized for continuous improvement.

The process is flexible enough to adapt to your unique situation while maintaining the structural discipline that delivers consistent, predictable outcomes. Whether you are launching a debut novel or revitalizing a backlist of 20 titles, this framework ensures we cover all the bases and maximize your return on investment at every stage.

My Process Promises

Everything Documented

Every step, decision, and result is recorded via email for your permanent records.

Data-Driven Decisions

No guesswork. Every decision is backed by research, analysis, and real performance data.

Full Transparency

You see everything I see. Regular reports with complete data and honest assessments.

Continuous Optimization

Campaigns are never set-and-forget. I test, refine, and improve continuously.

Your Approval Required

Nothing happens without your explicit approval. You are always in control.

The 5 Steps

Every Step Explained in Detail

01

Discovery and Assessment

Understanding Your Book, Your Market, and Your Goals

Everything begins with a thorough discovery phase. You will email me the details about your book, your goals, your previous marketing efforts (if any), your budget, and your vision for success. I will review everything carefully, conduct in-depth research into your genre and competitive landscape, analyze comparable titles and their marketing strategies, and develop a comprehensive assessment of your current position in the marketplace.

This is not a rushed process. I take the time to truly understand your book, your unique voice, your target audience, and your definition of success before making any recommendations. The discovery phase typically takes 5 to 7 business days, during which I will ask follow-up questions via email to ensure I have a complete picture of your situation.

At the end of this phase, you will receive a detailed Discovery Report via email that includes my analysis of your market position, identified opportunities and challenges, and preliminary strategic recommendations. This document becomes the foundation for everything that follows.

Book Analysis

Deep dive into your book's genre positioning, cover appeal, description effectiveness, and metadata quality.

Audience Research

Identification of your ideal reader demographics, behaviors, preferred platforms, and purchasing patterns.

Competitive Analysis

Study of comparable titles, their marketing strategies, pricing, rankings, and what is working in your niche.

Opportunity Mapping

Identification of untapped marketing channels, underserved keywords, and strategic positioning opportunities.

Deliverables
Discovery Report Market Analysis Competitive Landscape Review Audience Profile Document Initial Recommendations
02

Custom Strategy Development

Building Your Personalized Marketing Blueprint

Based on the discovery findings, I build a completely custom marketing strategy tailored specifically to your book, genre, audience, publishing path, budget, and goals. This is not a template or a generic plan. It is a comprehensive strategic document that covers every channel we will use, every tactic we will deploy, every timeline milestone, every budget allocation, and every expected outcome.

The strategy document is extensive and detailed. It typically runs 15 to 25 pages and includes specific campaign plans for each channel (advertising, social media, email, PR), creative direction guidelines, content calendars, keyword strategies, budget breakdowns with projected returns, and clear success metrics that we will use to evaluate performance.

You will receive this comprehensive plan via email with full explanations of each component so you understand exactly what we will be doing and why. I encourage questions, feedback, and discussion. Your input and explicit approval is required before we move forward to execution. This ensures you are fully aligned with and confident in the strategy before any money is spent.

Channel Strategy

Detailed plans for each marketing channel including Amazon, social media, email, and PR with specific tactics and timelines.

Budget Allocation

Precise breakdown of how every dollar will be spent across channels, with projected returns for each investment.

Timeline and Milestones

Week-by-week execution schedule with clear milestones, checkpoints, and decision points throughout the campaign.

Success Metrics

Clearly defined KPIs and benchmarks that we will use to measure campaign performance and guide optimization decisions.

Deliverables
Full Strategy Document (15-25 pages) Channel-by-Channel Plans Budget Allocation Breakdown Content Calendar Timeline with Milestones KPI Dashboard Setup
03

Campaign Build and Launch

Turning Strategy Into Action

With the strategy approved, I move into full execution mode. This is where all the planning translates into real-world action. Ad campaigns are built, tested, and launched across Amazon and other platforms. Social media content is created and scheduled. Email sequences are written, designed, and activated. Media pitches are crafted and sent to targeted contacts. Metadata is optimized across all retail platforms.

I handle all the technical setup, creative development, copywriting, platform management, and campaign configuration so you can focus on what you do best: writing your next book. Throughout this phase, I keep you informed via email about what is being launched, when, and what to expect. You will receive confirmation emails as each component goes live.

The launch phase is carefully choreographed to ensure maximum impact. Every element is timed to work together synergistically, creating a coordinated wave of visibility across multiple channels simultaneously. This is one of the most important advantages of working with a dedicated book marketing specialist rather than managing individual freelancers or agencies for each channel separately.

Ad Campaign Activation

Amazon, Facebook, Instagram, and BookBub campaigns go live with carefully researched targeting and optimized creative.

Content Creation

Social media posts, email templates, ad creatives, and promotional materials are produced and deployed across all channels.

Technical Setup

Tracking pixels, conversion monitoring, email automation, landing pages, and analytics dashboards are configured and tested.

Media Outreach

Personalized pitches go out to podcasts, bloggers, and media contacts timed to coincide with your campaign launch.

Deliverables
Live Ad Campaigns Social Media Content Calendar Email Automation Sequences Optimized Metadata Media Outreach Campaign Launch Confirmation Report
04

Monitoring, Testing, and Optimization

Where the Real Magic Happens

Launching a campaign is only the beginning. The real magic, the difference between good results and exceptional results, happens in the ongoing optimization phase. I monitor every metric daily, run continuous A/B tests on ad creatives, email subject lines, landing page copy, and audience targeting, adjust bids and budgets based on real-time performance data, and refine targeting to improve results week over week.

This phase is where my 10+ years of experience truly shine. I have developed an intuition for what data patterns mean and what adjustments will have the biggest impact, but I always back those instincts with rigorous testing before making significant changes. Every optimization decision is documented and explained in your regular performance reports.

You will receive detailed performance reports via email on a regular schedule (typically weekly during active campaigns). These reports include all key metrics, my analysis of what is working and what is not, specific actions I have taken or plan to take, and recommendations for any strategic adjustments. This level of reporting ensures you always know exactly how your campaigns are performing and what is being done to improve them.

Daily Monitoring

Every campaign is monitored daily for performance anomalies, budget pacing, and optimization opportunities.

A/B Testing

Continuous testing of ad creatives, headlines, targeting, email subject lines, and content formats to maximize performance.

Bid Optimization

Daily adjustment of advertising bids, budgets, and keyword targeting based on real performance data and trends.

Performance Reporting

Comprehensive weekly reports delivered via email with full data, analysis, insights, and recommended next steps.

Deliverables
Weekly Performance Reports A/B Test Results and Insights Optimization Log Budget Reallocation Updates Conversion Analysis Competitor Monitoring Updates
05

Scaling and Long-Term Growth

Building Sustainable, Career-Long Success

Once we have identified what is working best through rigorous testing and optimization, I scale those winning strategies to maximize your results. This phase is about building sustainable, long-term growth for your author career, not just short-term sales spikes that fade away after a few weeks.

We expand into new channels, build on proven audiences, develop cross-promotion strategies for your backlist, create evergreen marketing systems that continue delivering results for months and years, and establish you as a recognized authority in your genre. Book marketing is not a one-time event. It is an ongoing investment in your career, and this phase ensures that investment compounds over time.

For authors who continue working with me long-term, I provide quarterly strategy reviews where we assess overall performance, identify new opportunities, adjust our approach based on market changes and new data, and plan the next phase of growth. These reviews ensure that your marketing strategy continues to evolve and improve over time, keeping you ahead of the competition and top-of-mind with your readers.

Channel Expansion

Scaling winning strategies to new platforms and audiences while maintaining efficiency and return targets.

Backlist Integration

Cross-promoting your entire catalog to maximize the lifetime value of every reader you acquire.

Evergreen Systems

Building automated marketing systems that continue generating sales long after the initial campaign ends.

Quarterly Reviews

Comprehensive strategic assessments with updated plans, new opportunities, and refined growth roadmaps.

Deliverables
Scaling Strategy Document Long-Term Growth Roadmap Backlist Marketing Plan Evergreen Campaign Setup Quarterly Strategy Reviews Annual Performance Analysis
Communication

How We Stay Connected Throughout the Process

Clear, consistent, and documented communication is the backbone of every successful campaign. Here is how I keep you informed and involved at every stage.

Email-First Communication

All primary communication happens via email. This ensures that every discussion, recommendation, decision, and report is fully documented, underlined, and recorded for future reference. You will always have a complete, searchable record of our entire working relationship. I respond to all emails within 24 hours during active campaigns.

Regular Performance Reports

You will receive detailed performance reports on a regular schedule, typically weekly during active campaign phases and bi-weekly during maintenance phases. Each report includes all relevant metrics, my analysis and interpretation, actions taken, and recommended next steps. No jargon, no fluff, just clear, actionable information.

Calls on Particular Occasions

Phone and video calls are available and scheduled on particular occasions when real-time conversation adds genuine value. These include campaign kick-off discussions, complex strategy sessions, milestone reviews, and any situation where live dialogue provides more benefit than email. All call outcomes are documented via follow-up email.

Responsive and Accessible

I understand that questions arise and decisions need to be made quickly. During active campaigns, I am highly responsive to your emails and will always acknowledge receipt within a few hours, even if a detailed response requires additional time. You will never feel like your messages are going into a void.

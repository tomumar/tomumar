Who I Am

A Decade of Turning Authors Into Bestsellers

I am Tom Umar, a Book Marketing Specialist Expert who has spent over 10 years helping authors across every genre and publishing path achieve meaningful, lasting success in a rapidly evolving marketplace.

My journey into book marketing began with a simple observation: talented authors were writing incredible books, but far too many of those books were never reaching the readers who would love them. The publishing landscape can be overwhelming, filled with conflicting advice, expensive guesswork, and strategies that simply do not deliver results. I set out to change that by building marketing campaigns that are rooted in data, driven by strategy, and measured by real, tangible outcomes.

Every project I take on is built on three core principles: precision over guesswork, complete transparency at every step, and email-first communication that ensures everything is documented, underlined, and recorded for future reference. Phone and video calls are reserved for particular occasions when real-time conversation is genuinely necessary.

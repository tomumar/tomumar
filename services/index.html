Comprehensive Book Marketing Services

Every service is custom-tailored to your unique book, genre, audience, goals, and budget. There is no one-size-fits-all approach here. Each strategy is researched, planned, and executed from the ground up for maximum impact. Below is a detailed look at every service I offer.

01

Book Launch Campaigns

A successful book launch does not happen by accident. It requires months of careful planning, strategic positioning, and coordinated execution across multiple channels. I design and execute comprehensive launch campaigns that build anticipation well before your release date, create a powerful and sustained surge of sales and visibility during launch week, and maintain momentum long after the initial excitement fades. Every launch I manage follows a proven framework refined over 10+ years and dozens of successful launches, while still being customized to your specific book, genre, and target audience.

  • Pre-launch audience building, buzz generation, and ARC team coordination starting 60 to 90 days before release
  • Launch week sales surge coordination across all platforms including Amazon, Barnes and Noble, Kobo, and Apple Books
  • Post-launch sustained visibility strategy including ongoing advertising, email marketing, and social media content
  • Review generation strategy including Goodreads campaigns, BookBub features, and targeted reviewer outreach
  • Retailer optimization with A+ Content, editorial reviews, and strategic category and keyword placement
  • Media and podcast outreach timed to coincide with your launch for maximum publicity impact

  • Platform-specific content strategy for Instagram, TikTok (BookTok), Facebook, Twitter/X, and Pinterest
  • BookTok and Bookstagram viral content creation and growth campaigns designed for maximum organic reach
  • Community building through reader engagement, Q&A sessions, behind-the-scenes content, and interactive posts
  • Paid social advertising management with audience targeting, retargeting, and lookalike audience development
  • Influencer and BookToker collaboration outreach and partnership management
  • Monthly analytics reporting with engagement metrics, growth tracking, and content performance analysis

03

Email Marketing and List Building

Your email list is the single most valuable marketing asset you can own as an author. Unlike social media followers, your email subscribers are yours. No algorithm can take them away from you. I build, grow, and manage email marketing systems that turn casual readers into devoted fans who eagerly anticipate your next release, buy it on day one, and tell all their friends about it. From creating irresistible lead magnets and optimized signup forms to building sophisticated automated sequences and crafting compelling launch broadcasts, I handle every aspect of your email marketing.

  • Lead magnet creation including bonus chapters, short stories, character profiles, and exclusive content
  • Automated welcome sequences that immediately engage new subscribers and build anticipation
  • Launch broadcast campaigns with countdown sequences, early access offers, and exclusive bonuses
  • Advanced list segmentation based on reader preferences, purchase history, and engagement levels
  • A/B testing of subject lines, content formats, send times, and calls to action for maximum performance
  • Platform setup and management on ConvertKit, Mailchimp, MailerLite, or your preferred provider

05

Book SEO and Metadata Optimization

Your book's discoverability on Amazon and other retailers depends heavily on your metadata: your title, subtitle, description, backend keywords, and category selections. I optimize every single element of your book's metadata to ensure it ranks as highly as possible in search results, appears in the most relevant and advantageous categories, and converts browsers into buyers. This is often the highest-ROI activity in all of book marketing, yet most authors overlook it entirely or do it poorly.

  • Comprehensive keyword research using Publisher Rocket, KDP Rocket, and proprietary tools
  • BISAC category selection and strategic Amazon browse category optimization
  • Book description copywriting with conversion-optimized HTML formatting and A+ Content creation
  • Backend keyword optimization using all seven keyword slots for maximum discoverability
  • Competitive analysis to identify gaps, opportunities, and positioning advantages in your genre
  • Ongoing monitoring, refinement, and seasonal adjustments to maintain optimal performance

I leverage my extensive network of media contacts, built and nurtured over 10+ years in the industry, to secure meaningful placements that put you and your book in front of the right people at the right time. Every pitch is carefully crafted to match the outlet's specific audience, editorial style, and content needs.

  • Podcast booking on genre-relevant and general audience shows with interview preparation and talking points
  • Blog tour coordination and management with targeted outreach to genre-specific book bloggers
  • Press release writing, formatting, and distribution to relevant media outlets and industry contacts
  • Professional media kit creation including author bio, high-resolution photos, book summaries, and press quotes
  • Ongoing media relationship management and nurturing for long-term publicity opportunities
  • Award submission research and application management for relevant literary awards and honors

Frequently Asked Questions

I believe in complete transparency, so here are detailed, honest answers to the questions I receive most often from prospective clients. If your question is not covered here, please do not hesitate to email me at hello.tomumar@gmail.com.

Services and Expertise

Over the course of my 10+ year career, I have successfully marketed books across more than 15 genres including thriller, mystery, romance, science fiction, fantasy, literary fiction, memoir, self-help, business, health and wellness, children's books, young adult, historical fiction, horror, and poetry. While I have particularly deep expertise in commercial fiction and non-fiction, my data-driven approach means I can develop effective strategies for virtually any genre. Each genre has its own unique audience behaviors, marketing channels, pricing dynamics, and competitive landscape, and I tailor every campaign accordingly based on thorough research specific to your niche.

I work with authors across all publishing paths and bring the same level of dedication and expertise to every project regardless of how you publish. Self-published authors benefit from my comprehensive approach that covers everything from metadata optimization to multi-platform advertising. Traditionally published authors often need independent marketing support beyond what their publisher provides, and I create strategies that complement and amplify their publisher's efforts. Hybrid authors benefit from unified strategies that bridge both worlds. The fundamental principles of effective book marketing remain the same across all publishing paths, and my process is adaptable to each.

Absolutely, and this is actually one of my specialties. Many of my clients come to me with books that have already been published but are underperforming, have stalled in sales, or never received proper marketing in the first place. Revitalizing an existing title often involves a comprehensive audit of your current metadata, pricing, cover appeal, and competitive positioning, followed by a strategic marketing plan designed to reignite sales and reach entirely new audiences. Some of my most successful campaigns have been for books published months or even years before the client contacted me. It is never too late to give your book the marketing it deserves.

I offer both. While my most comprehensive results come from full-service, multi-channel campaigns where all the marketing elements work together synergistically, I also offer individual services for authors who have specific needs. For example, you might only need metadata optimization, or just Amazon advertising management, or a focused email marketing setup. During our initial email consultation, I will recommend the approach that makes the most sense for your specific goals and budget. I will always be honest about what level of service will deliver the best return on your investment.

Budget and Pricing

I work with a range of budgets and tailor every campaign to maximize the impact of whatever resources are available. I have achieved bestseller results with budgets as modest as $3,000 and have managed large-scale campaigns with budgets exceeding $20,000. During our initial consultation, I will provide honest, realistic recommendations about what is genuinely achievable within your budget and help you prioritize the strategies that will deliver the highest return on your investment. I will never recommend spending more than what makes strategic sense for your specific situation, and I will always be upfront about expected outcomes at different budget levels.

My fee structure varies depending on the scope of work and the services required. I typically work on a project-based fee for defined campaigns (such as book launches) or a monthly retainer for ongoing marketing management. Every project receives a detailed, custom quote that breaks down exactly what is included, what the costs are, and what results you can reasonably expect. There are no hidden fees, no surprise charges, and no vague "percentage of sales" arrangements. You will know exactly what you are paying for before we begin, and everything is documented in writing via email.

Yes. My management fees are separate from any advertising spend. The advertising budget goes directly to the platforms (Amazon, Facebook, BookBub, etc.) and is fully controlled and tracked by me on your behalf. You will see exactly how much is being spent on each platform, what results each platform is generating, and what the return on ad spend (ROAS) is for every campaign. I provide complete transparency into all advertising expenditures through detailed weekly or bi-weekly reports delivered via email.

Timeline and Results

Campaign durations vary depending on your goals and the type of marketing we are doing. A focused book launch campaign typically runs for 60 to 90 days including pre-launch preparation, launch week execution, and post-launch optimization. Ongoing marketing and growth campaigns run for 3 to 12 months or longer. I always recommend thinking of book marketing as a long-term investment rather than a one-time event, because the authors who see the greatest success are those who commit to consistent, strategic marketing over time. We will discuss timeline expectations in detail during the strategy development phase, and everything will be documented in our written communications.

Some results, particularly from paid advertising and metadata optimization, can begin to appear within the first one to two weeks. However, the most meaningful and sustainable results typically emerge over the first 30 to 90 days as campaigns are optimized and refined based on real performance data. I always set realistic expectations upfront and provide regular updates via email so you can track progress from day one. I will never promise overnight miracles or guarantee specific sales numbers, because no ethical marketer can do that. What I will commit to is doing everything strategically possible to maximize your results and providing complete transparency into what is working and what we are adjusting.

I do not guarantee specific sales numbers or bestseller placements, and you should be wary of any marketer who does. There are too many variables in book marketing that are outside any marketer's control, including market conditions, reader tastes, competitive activity, and algorithmic changes. What I do guarantee is that I will bring over 10 years of expertise, a proven process, rigorous data analysis, complete transparency, and an unwavering commitment to maximizing your results. My track record of 50+ bestselling authors, 4.2x average ROAS, and 98% client satisfaction rate demonstrates the consistent quality of my work across hundreds of campaigns.

Communication

I communicate primarily through email because it provides the highest level of documentation, transparency, and accountability for both of us. Every strategy discussion, every recommendation, every piece of feedback, every report, and every decision is recorded in writing and available for reference at any time. This ensures nothing is ever lost, forgotten, or misunderstood. It also allows me to provide more thoughtful, researched, and comprehensive responses than would be possible during a real-time phone conversation. The email-first approach has consistently been praised by my clients as one of the most valuable aspects of working with me.

Phone and video calls are absolutely available and are scheduled on particular occasions when real-time conversation provides genuine additional value. These occasions include complex strategy discussions that benefit from back-and-forth dialogue, campaign kick-off meetings, milestone reviews, and any situation where a live conversation will clearly be more productive than an email exchange. However, the foundation of our communication will always remain email to ensure that everything stays properly documented and on record. After every call, I send a detailed follow-up email summarizing the key points discussed and any decisions made, so there is always a written record.

Transparency is one of my core values, and my reporting reflects that commitment fully. You will receive detailed performance reports on a regular schedule, typically weekly during active campaign phases and bi-weekly during maintenance or scaling phases. These reports cover all key metrics including sales data, advertising performance (impressions, clicks, conversions, cost per click, ROAS), email marketing metrics (open rates, click rates, subscriber growth, revenue per subscriber), social media analytics (engagement rates, reach, follower growth, content performance), media coverage secured, and any other relevant data points specific to your campaign. Every report includes my analysis of what the data means, what actions I am taking or recommending, and clear next steps. Reports are delivered via email so they become a permanent part of our documented record.

Getting Started

Getting started is simple. Visit my contact page and fill out the form with details about your book, your goals, and your current situation. Alternatively, you can send an email directly to hello.tomumar@gmail.com. I will review your submission carefully, conduct preliminary research on your genre and market, and respond within 24 to 48 hours with an initial assessment and recommended next steps. There is no obligation and no pressure at any stage. I want to make sure I am the right fit for your project before we move forward, and I am always happy to answer any preliminary questions you might have before making a decision.

None whatsoever. Reaching out is completely free and carries absolutely no obligation. The initial consultation is my opportunity to understand your project, provide an honest assessment, and demonstrate the value I can bring. You are under no pressure to proceed at any point. Many authors reach out, have a productive email exchange, and then take time to think before deciding. I respect that completely and am happy to be patient while you make the right decision for yourself and your career.

The more information you can share, the more specific and helpful my initial response will be. Ideally, you should be ready to share:

  • Your book's title, genre, and a brief description of the content
  • Your publishing status and path (self-published, traditional, hybrid)
  • Your primary marketing goals and what success looks like to you
  • Your approximate budget range for marketing investment
  • Any previous marketing efforts and their results
  • Your existing platform size (social media, email list, website)
  • Your timeline and any upcoming deadlines or release dates

However, if you do not have all of this information ready, that is perfectly fine. I am happy to start the conversation with whatever you have and guide you through the rest during our discovery phase.
